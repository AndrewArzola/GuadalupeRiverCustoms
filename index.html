About the shop

Rooted on the Guadalupe

Guadalupe River Customs is a small motorcycle and fabrication shop in Seguin, Texas. Every job gets the same care, whether it's a full build or a tab welded on a bracket. You'll talk straight to the person doing the work.

Local riders can bring bikes to the shop. Parts from our store ship anywhere in the United States.
